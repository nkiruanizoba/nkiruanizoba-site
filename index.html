AI for game development

AI-assisted world building

Three concurrent AI initiatives for building large game environments: placing props, texturing buildings and catching performance problems in the level editor early, before a long build.

Target: roughly 10 to 15% workflow savings across art and level editing.

Developer productivity

Build diagnostics and branch stability

An LLM assistant that helps diagnose build failures, as part of broader work on branch stability metrics and interventions.

Impact: the broader branch stability work was associated with roughly a 15% reduction in build regressions over a year.

Multimodal AI for game QA

Knowing when not to ship

A multimodal LLM workflow that flagged localization and UI issues in in-game screenshots. It worked technically. Measured against the existing workflow and its real costs, the savings didn't justify building and maintaining it, so we stopped.

Impact: avoided putting an AI tool into production that the business case didn't support.

Reasoning-centered learning platform

A learning platform designed to assess reasoning, not accuracy. Rather than scoring answers as right or wrong, it focuses on how a learner is thinking and responds to that. The assessment system behind it is U.S. patent pending.

Insights engine

Sift

An AI-powered insights engine that turns unstructured feedback into traceable themes and trends. Sift answers questions in plain English and surfaces the relevant insights, with every finding linked back to the reviews that support it. Built on public Steam reviews, it tracks how player feedback changes across game updates and is validated against hand-labeled evals.
